Ain Sokhna, Egypt

Back to Projects

Project Overview

AZHA SEA FRONT is a premium coastal development located along the pristine shores of Ain Sokhna on the Red Sea. This exclusive waterfront project combines luxury residential and hospitality elements with thoughtfully designed public spaces to create an exceptional seaside destination.

The development features a range of high-end accommodations, dining venues, and recreational facilities, all oriented to maximize views of the sea and create a seamless connection between the built environment and the natural coastal landscape. The landscape design plays a crucial role in defining the character of the development, with carefully composed beachfront promenades, terraced gardens, and intimate courtyards that enhance the visitor experience.

As a landscape architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily focused on creating a sustainable and visually stunning coastal landscape that respects the natural environment while providing luxurious amenities for residents and guests. The design incorporates salt-tolerant native plants, water-efficient irrigation systems, and durable materials that can withstand the challenging coastal conditions while maintaining an elegant aesthetic that complements the architectural vision.
