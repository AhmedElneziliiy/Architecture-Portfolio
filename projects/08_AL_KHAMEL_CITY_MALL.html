AL KHAMEL CITY MALL

October 6th, Egypt

Back to Projects

Project Overview

AL KHAMEL CITY MALL is a premier shopping and entertainment destination located in October 6th City, Egypt. This modern commercial complex combines contemporary architectural design with thoughtful landscape elements to create an exceptional shopping experience for visitors.

The mall features a diverse mix of retail outlets, dining options, entertainment facilities, and public spaces designed to cater to the needs of the local community and visitors alike. The landscape design incorporates green spaces, water features, and outdoor seating areas that enhance the overall ambiance and provide pleasant environments for relaxation and social interaction.

As a landscape architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily contributed to creating an integrated landscape plan that complements the architectural elements while providing functional and aesthetically pleasing outdoor spaces. The design emphasizes sustainability, with careful consideration given to water conservation, the use of local materials, and the creation of comfortable microclimates throughout the exterior spaces.
