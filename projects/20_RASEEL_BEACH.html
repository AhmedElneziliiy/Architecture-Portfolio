Project Overview

RASEEL BEACH is a premium coastal development located in Dammam, Saudi Arabia. This exclusive waterfront project combines leisure, recreation, and hospitality elements to create a vibrant destination along the Arabian Gulf coastline.

The project features a carefully designed beachfront with a variety of amenities including swimming areas, water sports facilities, dining venues, and relaxation spaces, all integrated within a cohesive landscape design. The outdoor spaces are thoughtfully planned to maximize views of the sea while providing comfortable environments for visitors throughout the day and evening.

As a landscape architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily focused on creating a sustainable and visually stunning coastal landscape that respects the natural environment while providing exceptional recreational experiences for visitors. The design incorporates salt-tolerant native plants, durable materials that can withstand the challenging coastal conditions, and thoughtful lighting that enhances the nighttime experience while minimizing impact on the marine environment.
