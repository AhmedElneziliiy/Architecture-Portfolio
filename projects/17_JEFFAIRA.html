Project Overview

JEFFAIRA is a luxury coastal resort development located on Egypt's prestigious North Coast. This exclusive destination combines contemporary Mediterranean-inspired architecture with thoughtfully designed landscape elements to create an exceptional vacation experience for discerning travelers.

The resort features a collection of premium villas, elegant hotel accommodations, and world-class amenities, all set within a carefully crafted landscape that enhances the natural beauty of the coastal setting. The outdoor spaces are designed to provide a variety of experiences, from active recreation areas to intimate contemplative gardens, all while maximizing views of the Mediterranean Sea.

As a landscape architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily focused on creating a sustainable and visually stunning coastal landscape that respects the natural environment while providing luxurious amenities for residents and guests. The design incorporates drought-resistant Mediterranean plants, efficient water management systems, and high-quality materials that can withstand the challenging coastal conditions while maintaining an elegant aesthetic that complements the architectural vision.
