Project Overview

The DR.HASSAN PRIVATE VILLA project in El Shrouq City represents a sophisticated integration of contemporary architecture and elegant landscape design. This exclusive residence was designed to provide a luxurious living environment while maintaining a strong connection to nature and the surrounding landscape.

The landscape design for this prestigious property creates a series of interconnected outdoor spaces that serve as extensions of the interior living areas. From the formal entry garden to intimate courtyard spaces and expansive entertainment areas, each zone was meticulously planned to enhance the homeowner's lifestyle while providing year-round visual interest.

As the landscape architect for this project, Khaled Elnezily collaborated closely with the client to develop a personalized outdoor environment that reflects their aesthetic preferences and functional requirements. The design incorporates carefully selected plant materials, custom water features, and premium hardscape elements that create a cohesive and harmonious landscape that complements the architectural style of the residence.
