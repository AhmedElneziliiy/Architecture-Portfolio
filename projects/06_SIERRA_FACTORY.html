Project Overview

SIERRA FACTORY is an industrial facility project that demonstrates how thoughtful landscape design can enhance even the most functional of spaces. Located in an industrial zone in Egypt, this project combines practical considerations with aesthetic elements to create a more pleasant and productive work environment.

The landscape design for this industrial facility focuses on creating a green buffer around the perimeter, enhancing entry points, and providing comfortable outdoor break areas for employees. The design incorporates low-maintenance, drought-resistant plantings that can thrive in the challenging industrial environment while providing visual relief from the surrounding hardscape.

As a landscape architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily worked to balance functional requirements with environmental considerations, creating a design that improves the site's appearance, manages stormwater runoff, and provides shade and comfort for workers. The project demonstrates how even industrial sites can benefit from thoughtful landscape interventions that improve both functionality and user experience.
