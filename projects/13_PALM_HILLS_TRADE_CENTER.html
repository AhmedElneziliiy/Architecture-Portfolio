Project Overview

PALM HILLS TRADE CENTER is a premium commercial development located in the prestigious Zayed City area of Egypt. This sophisticated business complex combines elegant architectural design with thoughtfully planned outdoor spaces to create an exceptional environment for commerce and professional services.

The development features high-end office spaces, retail outlets, and amenity areas designed to meet the needs of modern businesses and their clients. The landscape design plays a crucial role in establishing the center's upscale identity, with carefully composed entry sequences, courtyard gardens, and outdoor meeting spaces that enhance the user experience while providing visual interest and environmental benefits.

As a landscape architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily focused on creating a cohesive and sophisticated outdoor environment that complements the architectural design while addressing practical considerations such as pedestrian circulation, shade, and climate adaptation. The design incorporates a refined palette of materials and plantings that reflect the premium positioning of the development while ensuring sustainability and ease of maintenance.
