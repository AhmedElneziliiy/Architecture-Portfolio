Project Overview

RASEEL RESORT is a luxurious resort development project located in Dammam, Saudi Arabia. This 80-acre masterpiece combines modern architectural elements with traditional Arabian influences, creating a unique destination for visitors seeking both relaxation and adventure.

The resort features a variety of accommodation options, from private villas to premium hotel suites, all designed to provide maximum comfort while maintaining a connection to the natural surroundings. The landscape design incorporates lush gardens, water features, and carefully planned outdoor spaces that enhance the guest experience.

As the lead Landscape Architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily focused on creating sustainable and visually stunning outdoor environments that complement the architectural elements while providing functional spaces for recreation and relaxation.
