Back to Projects

Project Overview

BLUE RESORT is a premium coastal resort development nestled along the pristine shores of Ain Sokhna on the Red Sea. This exclusive retreat combines contemporary architectural design with the natural beauty of the coastal landscape, creating an idyllic destination for travelers seeking both luxury and connection to nature.

The resort features a variety of accommodation options, from beachfront villas to elegant hotel suites, all designed to maximize views of the sea while providing privacy and comfort. The landscape design incorporates drought-resistant native plants, thoughtfully designed water features, and expansive outdoor spaces that enhance the guest experience.

As a landscape architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily focused on creating sustainable outdoor environments that complement the architectural elements while respecting the delicate coastal ecosystem. The design emphasizes the creation of comfortable microclimates through strategic shade structures, water elements, and plant selection, allowing guests to enjoy the outdoor spaces throughout the year despite the challenging desert climate.
