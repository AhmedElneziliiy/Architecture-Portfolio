CLEOPATRA MALL

October 6th, Egypt

Back to Projects

Project Overview

CLEOPATRA MALL is a luxury shopping and entertainment destination located in October 6th City, Egypt. This upscale commercial development combines Egyptian-inspired architectural elements with contemporary design to create a unique and memorable shopping experience.

The mall features premium retail spaces, gourmet dining options, entertainment venues, and elegantly designed public areas that cater to a discerning clientele. The landscape design plays a crucial role in establishing the mall's identity, with lush gardens, water features, and outdoor gathering spaces that reference Egypt's rich cultural heritage while providing comfortable and attractive environments for visitors.

As a landscape architect on this project, Khaled Elnezily contributed to creating a cohesive outdoor environment that enhances the architectural concept while addressing practical considerations such as pedestrian circulation, shade, and climate adaptation. The design incorporates a sophisticated palette of materials and plantings that reflect the luxury positioning of the mall while ensuring sustainability and ease of maintenance.
