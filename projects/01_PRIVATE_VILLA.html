Project Overview

This PRIVATE VILLA project in Borg El Arab showcases a harmonious blend of contemporary architecture and thoughtful landscape design. The residence was designed to provide both luxury and functionality while maintaining a strong connection to the surrounding environment.

The landscape design for this private residence focuses on creating a series of outdoor living spaces that extend the interior experience into the garden. From intimate courtyards to expansive entertainment areas, each space was carefully crafted to enhance the homeowner's lifestyle while providing visual interest throughout the seasons.

As the landscape architect for this project, Khaled Elnezily worked closely with the client to understand their specific needs and preferences, resulting in a personalized outdoor environment that complements the architectural elements while providing privacy, shade, and beauty. The design incorporates a thoughtful selection of native and adapted plants, sustainable water features, and high-quality materials that will age gracefully over time.
